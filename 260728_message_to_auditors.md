**Subject: NRMM audit analysis, a provisional look and a few questions**

Hello,

I've made solid progress on the analysis of the NRMM audit records, and I'd like to share where it's got to and check a few things with you.

I've attached an interactive dashboard. It's a single file, so just open it in a browser, nothing to install. There's a **Glossary** view if any of the terms are unfamiliar, and a **Data queries** view listing the things I'd like your help with. Most of the numbers hold up well, and there's a clear picture emerging.

**Two findings I'm fairly confident about.** Sites that register their machines bring cleaner machines through the gate than sites that don't: 89% of registered machines already met the required standard when first seen, against 81% of unregistered ones. And fleet renewal roughly doubled when the requirements stepped up in September 2020. Interestingly, both groups replace machines at about the same rate once they're on site, so the difference is in what turns up, not in what happens after an inspection.

**One area I've had to put a hold on, and it's where I'd most value your view.** The Constant Speed group looks as though it hasn't improved at all since 2019, which didn't sit right. Looking closer, all 218 generators recorded at Stage V carry Engine Type "Variable", while Stage IIIA generators audited in the same years are 80% "Constant". Since the group is defined by that field, generators appear to leave the group at the moment they upgrade, which would make the group look stuck whether or not it actually is. Read the other way, the same group's average stage climbs steadily from 2022 onwards.

I'm genuinely not assuming either reading is correct. Hybrid, flywheel and flybrid units in your data split 37 "Constant" to 51 "Variable", which looks like a deliberate distinction rather than an accident, so a blanket rule on my side would probably be wrong. The Data queries view shows both readings side by side. Until I hear from you I've left the figures as recorded and flagged the group as provisional, so please treat anything labelled Constant Speed as not yet reliable. Everything else in the dashboard is unaffected.

**A few smaller questions**, all in the attached note (and thank you for confirming that "Electric" means zero emission, which is now reflected): what "Uncertified" and "Pending" are intended to mean; whether crushers should ever be constant speed; when TAN capture was introduced, since I use it to follow machines between sites; and whether "Inappropriate for Audit" should be treated as out of scope.

None of this is a criticism of the recording. In most cases I expect there's a practice or a piece of guidance I simply don't know about, and a short answer from you would save me guessing and quite possibly getting it wrong. I've also fixed a handful of things at my end that were my own doing, so those aren't in the list.

Happy to work to whatever convention you intend. If it's easier to talk it through than to write it down, I'd welcome half an hour whenever suits you.

Thanks very much,
