## Context

This is a exploratory data science project in R to evaluate a Low Emissions Zone (LEZ) by analysing data from 16,251 machine audits. The LEZ requires individual machines to meet specified emissions standard that vary with location, date of use and type of machine. If it does not meet the standard, then it must be replaced or removed, or else face sanction.

The data are cross sectional samples of the machinery fleet in the period 2016-2025 inclusive. Each audit determine the machines emission standard stage (I, II, IIIA, IIIB, IV, or V, where V is the cleanest).

The audits are classified:

- **Cold-engaged** — machines not registered with the LEZ, itself illegal, which we use as the Counter Factual sample.

- **Warm-engaged** — machines were registered with the NRMM LEZ (treatment group subject to policy)

The analytical objectives are to:

1\. import and cleanse the data;

2\. determine which data represents a **counterfactual, CF** and which **actual, AT**;

3\. derive **compliance rates e-bar (**$\bar c$), for CT and AT;

4\. derive **rate of replacement of equipment,** $\bar p$, for CT and AT, using a **Markov model**;

5\. develop scenarios for **future take-up** of compliant equipment.

6\. for each period and sub-group, determine an effective **Emissions Factor, EF** representative of the fleet average.

## Key definitions and parameters

#### S, sub-group, the set of machines audited in a given LEZ period and group

Each **S, sub-group** therefore references a specified machine group and LEZ period or sub-period, e.g. `$S_CAZ^A1$` refers to those machines audited in the CAZ during sub-period A1.

#### c-bar ($\bar c$), proportion of compliant machines

$\bar c$ is the percentage of all audited machines whose emissions stage at first audit or in follow-on action (columns `Initial Emissions Stage` and `Final Emissions Stage` in file `audits.txt`) met minimum required threshold for that date and location, as set out in schema.md.

The *change* in $\bar c$ between phases divided by elapsed time gives a compliance improvement rate in percentage points per year:

$$Δ \bar c / Δt = (\bar c_{later} − \bar c_{earlier}) / years$$

This is a flow measure expressing how fast non-compliance is declining, directly answering whether the LEZ is working and at what pace.

#### p-bar ($\bar p$), rate of replacement of equipment

$\bar p$ is the **annual average machine replacement probability**, to be derived using a Weighted Least Squares regression of fleet mean stage against year or fractional year, weighted by sub-group, S, sample size, assuming machines are replaced using the highest stage on the market at the time, known to be real-world behaviour.

#### Modelling approach

The modelling approach is based on Markov modelling, with phase-specific Markov transition matrices (P1–P4); see plans/schema.md for full specification.
